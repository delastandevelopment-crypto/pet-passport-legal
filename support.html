Pet Passport Support

Pet Passport keeps your pet's health and travel records on your iPhone. If you need help, contact us at delastandevelopment@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

Common questions

Where is my data stored? On your phone only. Pet Passport has no account and no cloud service. Backups are saved only where you choose.

How do I back up or move to a new phone? Open the Home tab, choose Backup, and save the file to iCloud Drive or Files. On the new phone, choose Restore and pick that file.

Why didn't my reminder go off? Check that notifications are allowed for Pet Passport in your iPhone settings.

Is this an official pet passport? No. Pet Passport is a record organizer. Always check your destination's requirements with your vet or the official authority.
