Pet Passport Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

In short: Pet Passport keeps everything on your phone. We do not have accounts, servers, or analytics, and we do not receive any of your information.

1. Who we are

Pet Passport is an app for recording your pets' vaccines, checkups, paperwork, medications, travel, costs and vet details. It is published by DELASTAN ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy can be sent to delastandevelopment@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The app has no sign-in, no account, and no connection to a server that we run. Nothing you enter in the app is sent to us or to anyone else by the app itself.

3. Where your information is stored

Everything you add to Pet Passport is stored on your phone only. This includes pet details, vaccine and checkup records, medications, trips, costs, photos, and scanned paperwork. It is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Deleting the app removes this information from your phone.

4. Photos and paperwork

Pet Passport can use your camera or photo library, and can open PDF files, only when you choose to add a photo or paperwork. Pet Passport saves a copy of each item inside the app. It does not upload these files anywhere. When you scan a paperwork photo, the text is read on your phone using Apple's Vision framework. The photo and the text it contains are not sent anywhere.

5. Backups

When you choose "Save a backup," Pet Passport creates a file on your phone and opens the share sheet. You decide where the file goes, such as iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or another app. That service handles the file under its own privacy policy. Backup files contain all of your Pet Passport information, so keep them somewhere private.

6. Sharing a pet profile

When you choose "Share profile (PDF)," Pet Passport creates a PDF on your phone and opens the share sheet. The PDF is sent only where you send it, such as to a pet sitter or vet. It can include the pet's vet and insurance details, so share it only with people you trust.

7. Face ID and passcode lock

If you turn on the optional lock, Pet Passport asks your phone to check Face ID or your passcode before the app opens. Your phone performs this check. Pet Passport never receives or stores your face data or fingerprint data. It only stores whether the lock is on or off.

8. Reminders

Reminders for due vaccines, checkups and trips are scheduled on your phone as local notifications. They do not use an internet connection, and they are not sent by us.

9. Finding a vet

The "Find a vet nearby" button opens your phone's maps app with a search for veterinarians. Pet Passport does not receive your location or the search results. Your maps app handles that search under its own privacy policy.

10. Third parties

Pet Passport does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking software from any third party, and it does not sell or share information.

11. Children

Pet Passport is for pet owners, not for children to use alone. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

12. Your choices

You control all of your information. You can edit or delete any record in the app, turn the Face ID lock on or off, and delete the app to remove everything it has stored on your phone. Backup and PDF files that you saved or shared are your own copies and are not removed by deleting the app.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.

14. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact delastandevelopment@gmail.com.
